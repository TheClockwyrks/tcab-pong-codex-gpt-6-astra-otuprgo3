# Carom

The angle is everything. Carom is a quick, top-down paddle duel with a twist: every moving strike can curve the ball. Take on the machine in Solo, or challenge a friend to a local Versus match on one keyboard.

The first serve gives you a moment to find your footing. Meet the ball in the middle of your paddle for a clean return, or catch it at the edge to send it climbing. Each paddle hit makes the rally faster. Move as you connect and your shot carries spin, bending its path after it leaves you.

Two amber obstacles divide the court into opportunities. A safe return can become a bank shot; a wall bounce can turn into a surprising approach. Read the curve, move into position, and make the next angle yours.

First to eleven takes the match, but you need a two-point lead. At ten-all, every return matters. Keep your nerve through match point, then jump straight into a rematch.
